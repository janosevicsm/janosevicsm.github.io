01 - The problem

Machines change hands slowly.

Buying a used press or a machining centre usually means digging through scattered dealer sites, in several languages, then chasing sellers by phone.

eMachy puts new and used industrial machines in one place, in six languages: buyers contact sellers directly, and sellers get leads in a built-in inbox instead of paying commission.
